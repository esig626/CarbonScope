# Stationary validation

The tracer is 100% `#111111` glucose_ext (U-13C6), with 100% `#0000`
aspartate and no natural-abundance correction. There is no unlabelled glucose
dilution.

## Deterministic upstream layer

FluxEMU/mfapy produces exactly M+6 for glucose_c, G6P, F6P, and FBP; exactly
M+3 for DHAP, GAP, BPG, 3PG, 2PG, PEP, and pyruvate; and exactly M+2 for
AcCoA. The explicit map-level positional checks are in
[`GLYCOLYSIS_ATOM_MAPS.md`](GLYCOLYSIS_ATOM_MAPS.md).

## Independent TCA comparison

`independent_tca_mids.csv` is generated by the frozen direct 176-state
isotopomer balance solver after changing only its acetyl-CoA input to 100%
`#11`; it imports neither FluxEMU nor mfapy. `stationary_mids.csv` compares
that result with the complete FluxEMU/mfapy glucose-to-TCA calculation.

| Metabolite | MID M+0 through maximum mass, independent = FluxEMU/mfapy |

| citrate | `0, 0, 0.5, 0, 0.25, 0.1666666667, 0.0833333333` |
| AKG | `0, 0, 0.5, 0.125, 0.25, 0.125` |

Every reported MID is finite, nonnegative, and normalized. The regression
comparison has an absolute tolerance of `1e-12` for the stationary independent
and mfapy vectors.

## Recirculation established from positional state

The independent isotopomer state identifies citrate positions 4 and 5 as the
two pure-M+2 acetyl-CoA positions in `dcbfea`; citrate positions 1, 2, 3, and
6 originate from OAC. Since aspartate is wholly unlabelled, any label in that
OAC-derived set is direct positional evidence of label that has traversed the
cycle and returned to citrate. The independent state gives:

| Positional classification | Fraction |
| --- | ---: |
| citrate M+2 with all four OAC-derived positions unlabelled | 0.5 |
| citrate M+3 or higher with at least one returned labelled OAC position | 0.5 |
| citrate M+4 or higher with at least two returned labelled OAC positions | 0.5 |
| glutamate M+3 or higher | 0.5 |

This supports the recirculation interpretation without assigning a “turn
number” solely from an MID mass.
